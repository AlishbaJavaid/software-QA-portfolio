01 · About

Quality isn't a phase, it's a habit

I'm a Software QA Engineer with over five years of experience testing web and mobile applications in Agile environments. I work across the full stack of quality — manual and functional testing, REST API validation, performance and load testing, and direct database verification — rather than stopping at the UI.

Most recently, as Senior QA Engineer at CyberMart, I tested the Cybermart eCommerce platform end-to-end — buyer and seller workflows across product catalog, cart, checkout, payments, and order management — backed by Postman/Swagger API checks and JMeter load testing, and I've helped cut user-reported bugs by 30%. Across my career I've tested 6+ live products spanning eCommerce, matrimonial, travel, and social/experience-booking apps — and I write Playwright automation scripts in JavaScript/TypeScript for smoke and regression coverage.
